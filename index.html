Our Unique Approach

Multi-Resolution Omics Pyramid

Our training methodology integrates multiple levels of biological resolution, providing a comprehensive understanding of modern omics technologies:

🔬 Spatial Transcriptomics

Tissue-level gene expression with spatial context

Cell-type specific expression profiles
